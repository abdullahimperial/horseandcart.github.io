Privacy Policy

What stays in your browser, what reaches service providers, and the choices you have.

Effective and last updated: November 5, 2026

The essentials

The financial tools calculate in your browser. Their entries and imported sample files are not uploaded to us. Course notes and preferences can remain on your device. GitHub Pages, our website host, records visitors’ IP addresses for security. If you choose to email us, we receive the information you send.

Use fictional examples. Please do not provide banking credentials, account numbers, Social Insurance Numbers, identifiable financial statements or sensitive health information.

1. Who is responsible

Abdullah Mohiuddin operates Let’s Fix Money in Alberta, Canada and is the contact for privacy questions, access or correction requests, deletion requests and complaints: abdullah@letsfixmoney.com. Include “Privacy” in the subject and describe your request without sending identity documents or sensitive financial details.

3. Providers, external links and location

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub is a US-based provider and may process information in the United States and other countries described in its policies. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement for its handling of visit information.

Sending an email uses external email providers; their systems and backups may process messages outside Canada. Contact the privacy contact above if you need details about how a particular inquiry would be handled before sending it. Foreign processing may be subject to the laws of the country involved. The tools do not transmit their financial entries through these providers.

External resources open another organization’s website. That organization’s privacy practices apply there. We do not sell your inquiry information or use it for an unrelated marketing purpose. Service providers may handle information to deliver hosting or email, and information may be disclosed where legally required.

4. Retention and security

Browser notes and settings have no automatic expiry set by this site; you control them using the options below or your browser’s site-data settings. Local tool sessions are temporary, while downloaded files and printed copies remain until you delete or dispose of them. We cannot retrieve, correct or erase a file that exists only on your device.

Correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the matter and meet applicable recordkeeping or legal obligations. You may ask for deletion of information we hold; legal requirements, an ongoing matter or provider backup retention may limit immediate deletion. GitHub controls retention of its hosting logs under its own policies.

Keep sensitive information out of the tools and emails. Protect your device and email account, and avoid saving private notes on shared devices. No internet service, email system or device can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Your choices and browser data

You can browse without contacting us, use sample data, and leave optional fields blank. You can request access to or correction of information we hold, ask about retention or deletion, withdraw consent where applicable, and raise a privacy concern using the contact above. We may need proportionate information to verify a request. Applicable legal rights and exceptions govern how it is handled.

To remove locally saved learning data, first download any workbook you want to keep and close other course tabs so they do not save the notes again. The button below removes this site’s course notes, answers, progress and display preferences from this browser. It does not delete downloaded files, sent emails, hosting logs or unsaved work in another open tab.

Your browser’s site-data controls can also remove locally saved information. For unresolved concerns about an Alberta organization, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta.

6. Young learners and changes

Young learners should use fictional examples with a parent, guardian or educator and avoid entering identifying details. We do not invite children to send personal financial information. If a child has emailed information that should be removed, contact us.

If the site introduces online accounts, direct form submission, analytics, marketing subscriptions or other new processing, this notice and the relevant collection notices will be updated before the feature is used, with consent obtained where required.
