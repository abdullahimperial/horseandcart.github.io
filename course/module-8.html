Core lesson

The idea

The book presents giving back as a broad idea: automate charity, seek impactful charity, consider the ethics of investments, create jobs, lift up family and friends, improve opportunities for children, volunteer, support causes and support small businesses. It emphasizes that giving is not limited to money.

Giving can be planned and automated.Impact can include education, empowerment and job creation.Money can be considered alongside personal ethical values.Time, knowledge, support and community involvement can also be forms of giving.
Reflection

Apply it to your situation

If your financial position gave you more money, time or flexibility, what kind of positive impact would matter most to you?
Your action

Choose one realistic form of giving—money, time, knowledge, support or community action—to put into practice.
