Understand Your Financial Life

Some events are outside your control. Other financial choices are decisions you make. This module uses the opening game from the book to explore that distinction.

1 · LearnUnderstand the idea behind the Financial Life game.
2 · ExperiencePlay through major financial-life decisions.
3 · ReflectRecord what you noticed and choose one action.
Lesson

Chance versus decision

The book begins with a game in which a die represents events assumed to be outside your control, while “decide” gives you alternatives that affect your simulated net worth.

The point is not to predict your life.
The exercise asks you to notice what you can influence and how repeated choices can accumulate over time.

The book suggests playing the game before reading and again afterward to see whether your thinking changes.
