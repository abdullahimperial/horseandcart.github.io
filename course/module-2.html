Put the Horse Before the Cart

The horse-and-cart analogy asks whether the order of your financial decisions is making the journey easier or harder.

Core lesson

The idea

The book uses the horse and cart to argue for changing the sequence of some purchases: earn, save and prepare before spending or taking on unnecessary financing. Its car example contrasts repeated financing with buying a used vehicle, making payments to yourself, and later buying upfront. The chapter also asks readers to look beyond advertised prices toward actual costs and to compare income with expenses.
