Your route through the book

Understand → Save → Build resilience → Preserve → Grow → Transfer → Give

  1. Understand your starting point

    Separate the choices you can make from the circumstances you inherit. Explore whether income and spending leave breathing room.

    Begin with Financial Life →Try the Money Spiral →

  2. Put the horse before the cart

    Think about the obligations pulling on your income and the time a purchase costs.

    Learn the horse-and-cart idea →Try True Cost of a Purchase →

  3. Save through the four pillars

    Remove leakages, reduce living costs, investigate support and change habits. Apply each pillar to each expense.

    Learn the saving pillars →Explore an example financial report →

  4. Build your emergency system

    Consider accessible cash, essential costs, income risks and realistic alternative support—not just a single savings number.

    Explore emergency resilience →Review the emergency assessment →

  5. Preserve what you build

    Inspect the risks, borrowing costs and purchasing decisions that can undo progress.

    Learn about preservation →Map sinks and taps →

  6. Grow deliberately

    Connect surplus with goals, time horizons and risk. Learn what to verify before directing money into investments.

    Explore growth planning →Review the growth checks →

  7. Transfer, give and tell your story

    Help the next generation, consider the wider good and reflect on your own milestones.

    Begin transfer and giving lessons →Reflect on your milestones →
Keep the learning concrete. After each activity, record one insight, one assumption to verify and one next action. Course notes are saved in your browser when storage is available; CSV apps clear on reload, so download their reports if you want to keep them.
