The author’s experience

From monthly to annual prepaid

Abdullah previously paid $25 per month, then switched to a cheaper annual Koodo plan. His book describes 32 GB for the entire year, supplied upfront, plus unlimited Canada-wide calls and texts.

The advance payment covered that phone service until renewal. It also meant managing the data allowance across the full year.

The book’s earlier $26 figure was an approximate monthly comparison. The author has since clarified $25; the exact historical annual charge and tax treatment are not confirmed here. Today’s offer below is a separate comparison.

Listed Koodo offer · checked 9 November 2026

365 days, paid upfront

$159 per person / 365 days

  • 40 GB for the whole year at 4G speed.
  • Unlimited Canada-wide calling.
  • Available for new activations only.

That is a $13.25 monthly average before tax. Assuming 5% Alberta GST: $166.95 upfront, about $13.91/month. Separate SIM, activation or other fees are excluded.

Koodo calls this a 365-day plan. Data is provided upfront; unused annual data does not carry over. Its listed $25 prepaid alternative renews every 30 days, which differs from calendar-month billing. Features and data allowances differ between plans.
