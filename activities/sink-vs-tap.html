CHAPTER 5 · FOLLOW THE MONEY

Sink vs. Tap

Does a purchase drain your money, bring money back, or do a little of both? Trace its outcomes before deciding.

1. Pick a decision. List outcomes that take money away (sinks) and bring money back (taps). 2. Compare their size. Add optional monthly estimates. 3. Decide what to check or change.
