A frugal household budget

Two adults and one child. Explore a sample, change the assumptions, and build a monthly report.

What this truly is: a browser-only worksheet with fictional, editable example amounts. It does not search prices, find the cheapest offers, calculate eligibility or refresh market data. Reloading resets the example; it does not update the figures. The source links below are places to verify information, not evidence for the fictional amounts.

Household assumptions

Edmonton, Alberta example; two adults and one school-age child; rented housing; transit and walking; no car, debt or paid childcare. These are choices for this exercise, not a claim that this budget is sufficient or available for every household.

A low-income household may qualify for support, but eligibility for every program cannot be assumed. Housing subsidies may have waitlists, and some supports overlap or cannot be combined.

Monthly income

Benefits start at $0 because they have not been calculated. Replace this with a verified amount. For a quarterly payment, divide by 3; for an annual amount, divide by 12. A monthly average does not mean the money arrives monthly.

Do not count a discounted service as cash income as well as reducing its expense.

Your monthly report

Enter a non-negative amount in every numeric field to update the report.

This report is calculated from the values shown, not live market data. A surplus is a planning result, not proof that costs are realistic or benefits are payable. Savings and reserves are allocations, not consumption expenses.

Sources and places to check

No external source was used to price the fictional sample. These links are verification resources; opening this page does not retrieve or check their current contents. Record your own quotes and verification dates before using real figures.

  • CRA child and family benefits calculator — estimate benefits using the household’s income, child age and other details.
  • Canada child benefit — official payment calculation information.
  • Alberta Child and Family Benefit — eligibility and amounts.
  • City of Edmonton — check current transit assistance, recreation access and local program information.
  • Government of Alberta — check housing, childcare and health-support programs.

Limits and privacy

Educational example only, not personalized financial, tax, legal or benefit advice. It excludes individualized taxes, disability-related needs and other household-specific costs unless you add them to an existing line. Check fees, deposits, contracts, availability, eligibility and payment timing. All fields stay in page memory; nothing is sent or saved automatically. Printed or saved reports contain your entries. Do not enter account numbers or sensitive personal information.
