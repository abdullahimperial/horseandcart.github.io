The story behind the framework

A lived financial journey

The book follows the author’s experiences with affordability, family life and immigration, then develops ideas around saving, resilience, preservation, growth, generational wealth and giving.

The horse-and-cart metaphor asks whether your income is helping move you forward or being pulled back by debt, high costs and habits.
