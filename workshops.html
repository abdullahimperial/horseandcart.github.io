A proposed educational workshop for workplaces, community groups and financial-literacy programs, based on the book and its interactive companion.

Pilot format under development. Dates, fees, delivery arrangements and public contact details are not yet set. Prepare an inquiry to discuss a session once the contact channel is available.

A proposed session

  1. Understand: explore the horse-and-cart metaphor and money spiral.
  2. Practice: apply the four saving pillars to a fictional household.
  3. Compare: try Sink vs. Tap or True Cost of a Purchase.
  4. Plan: identify questions to verify and one practical next action.

Sessions would use fictional examples rather than collecting participants’ financial statements.

What participants could take away

  • A shared vocabulary for financial decisions.
  • An expense-by-four-pillars worksheet.
  • Questions about debt and emergency resilience.
  • A short action plan with a review date.

Educational discussion, not individual financial advice, certification or a promise of savings.
