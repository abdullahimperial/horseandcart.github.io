About the author & project

Real experiences.
Questions worth exploring.

Abdullah Mohiuddin is the author of Personal Finance Story: From Fighting Affordability to Generational Wealth.

Abdullah Mohiuddin

In the book, Abdullah describes growing up in Karachi, studying mechanical engineering and completing postgraduate education in Turkey and the UAE before moving to Alberta.

His family’s experiences with immigration, affordability, young children and home ownership shaped the practical questions explored in the manuscript.

He writes from personal experience. This companion presents those lessons as educational material and does not imply a financial-adviser designation.

Explore the book →

What this companion does

  • Introduces ideas through a visual course.
  • Uses games to examine chance and choice.
  • Turns examples into activities and calculations.
  • Connects transaction evidence to suggested next steps.

Canadian examples reflect the manuscript’s context. Program rules, tax treatment and financial circumstances vary.

How to use the work

Explore, verify, adapt.

The tools state assumptions and distinguish observed evidence from questions that still need checking. They are under construction and have not undergone an independent financial-content review.

The CSV and purchase apps process entries in the browser. They do not connect to banks. The course can save notes and learning progress locally; downloadable reports may contain the information entered.
