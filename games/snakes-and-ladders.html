How to play Roll to move. Ladders lift you; snakes send you back. Reach 88 with an exact roll. The original 11 × 8 board and event positions are preserved.
●

Green event squares show opportunities; coral event squares show setbacks. Each has an icon and label. Follow the numbers in a zigzag, starting at the bottom left. On small screens, swipe across the board.

Change background & align the grid

Use artwork with 11 columns and 8 rows, numbered like this board. Images keep their proportions. Drag the pink handles along the top and left to align each column and row with your artwork. A different board layout cannot use these event positions.
