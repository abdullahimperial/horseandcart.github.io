Snakes & Ladders

Build momentum. Watch for setbacks. Find your way to square 100.

🎲
How to play Roll to move. Ladders lift you; snakes send you back. Reach 100 with an exact roll. The current print-edition 10 × 10 board is used. Square 100 represents $1,000,000. On square 7, agree on a lower destination: that printed snake ends outside the numbered grid.
●

The book artwork and its colours are preserved. The optional high-contrast grid shows labelled events. Follow the numbers in a zigzag, starting at the bottom left. On small screens, swipe across the board.

Change background & align the grid

Use artwork with 10 columns and 10 rows, numbered like this board. Images keep their proportions. Drag the pink handles along the top and left to align each column and row with your artwork. A different board layout cannot use these event positions.
