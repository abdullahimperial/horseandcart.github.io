A simplified book game: annual career outcomes represent savings or debt, not gross salary.

Rules & timing

Amounts follow the manuscript. For college, outcomes 1, 2, 3 and 6 cover study costs: record no education cost, debt or leftover savings. Outcome 4 uses the selected degree’s annual loan amount; outcome 5 subtracts $5,000 per year for four years (ages 18–21). Age 22 is a transition year with no score change; roll for a career outcome at age 23. Trades or work savings apply from ages 18–22. Match each die number to the same-numbered board outcome. Career outcomes are annual game-score changes, not salaries; outcomes 5 and 6 represent recurring shortfalls of $5,000 and $10,000 per year. The six die results have equal chances in this game; they do not represent real-world career or income probabilities. Transport starts at 24; car financing lasts seven years, from 24–30. Relationship spending continues after age 30 only if you marry. Children and RESP costs start at 32 and 33. A mortgage runs for 25 years, followed by the book’s $450,000 home-value addition at 62. The game continues through age 65.

These timing conventions make annual costs apply once per year. This is a learning game, not a real balance sheet or forecast.
