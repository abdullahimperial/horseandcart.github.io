Your journey

A simplified book game: annual career outcomes represent savings or debt, not gross salary.

Rules & timing

Amounts are fixed to the manuscript. Study costs run for four years; trades or work savings run from 18–22. Career outcomes run from 23–62. Car financing lasts seven years. Relationship spending continues after age 30 only if you marry. Children and RESP costs start at 32 and 33. A mortgage runs for 25 years, followed by the book’s $450,000 home-value addition at 62.

These timing conventions make annual costs apply once per year. This is a learning game, not a real balance sheet or forecast.
