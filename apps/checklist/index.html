Where could you focus next?

Review your habits, see a scorecard, and choose three manageable actions.

A reflection tool, not a financial-health grade. Each applicable practice has equal weight: Yes = 2, Partly = 1, Not yet = 0. Not applicable is excluded. Unanswered questions are excluded too, so an incomplete score is provisional. The score does not measure wealth, income, eligibility or financial security. Use fictional answers during construction.

Now measure the result

Optional monthly estimates, following the book’s worksheet. Leave unknown amounts blank. Benefits add to income; savings reduce expenses. Avoid counting the same saving twice.
