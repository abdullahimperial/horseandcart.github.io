The four pillars

Remove unnecessary costs, find suitable lower-cost options, check support and free resources, and build useful spending habits. Choose what fits your circumstances; necessary care, safety and personal value matter.

Suggestions are prompts to investigate, not guaranteed savings or eligibility decisions. Canadian tax examples are identified. All amounts use CAD; changing currency keeps your numbers and does not convert them.
