Sales tax & deductions · edit assumptions

Use the applicable sales-tax rate and effective deductions for the earnings you are modeling. The prefilled rates are fictional examples, not official current rates.

Income tax is progressive. CPP/CPP2 and EI have exemptions, earnings limits and annual caps, so a flat percentage is a simplification. If modeling extra earnings after reaching a cap, an incremental deduction may be zero. Quebec uses QPP and may include QPIP; self-employed deductions differ. Adjust assumptions rather than treating this as a payroll calculator.

Ask whether the purchase protects essentials, supports earning, brings meaningful value, or could be delayed. A depreciating item can be useful; appreciation alone does not make something affordable.

This estimate covers the upfront purchase only. Financing interest, maintenance, insurance, future resale, existing bills and money needed for essentials are outside this calculation. Working-time equivalents do not tell you how long saving will take.
