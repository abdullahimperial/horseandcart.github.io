This projection applies your assumed return; it does not predict investment performance. Actual returns vary and may be negative. Fees, taxes and inflation are excluded. This model assumes no withdrawals; withdrawal timing would affect actual outcomes.

The blue center represents principal; the surrounding amber layer represents accumulated profit. With losses, the blue snowball shrinks. The snowball’s area grows with investment value, up to a size limit for readability. Its colored areas show the principal/profit proportions; the numbers show exact calculated values.

Why the snowball? Reinvested returns can earn further returns. Time makes that compounding visible, but a smooth downhill animation does not represent real market performance. Consider emergency savings, expensive debt and your time horizon before investing.
